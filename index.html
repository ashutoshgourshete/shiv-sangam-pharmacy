About Our Institution

Kranti Sangam Foundation's
OM SS Pune College of Physiotherapy

Established under the visionary aegis of Kranti Sangam Foundation, OM SS Pune College of Physiotherapy is dedicated to shaping skilled, compassionate, and globally competent physiotherapists. Situated in the serene and well-connected educational hub of Urali Kanchan, Pune, our campus blends academic rigor with clinical excellence.

Physiotherapy is an essential pillar of modern medical recovery, rehabilitation, and preventive health. Our institution offers state-of-the-art anatomy dissection halls, physiology labs, kinesiotherapy wards, electrotherapy units, and direct clinical postings with multi-speciality hospitals across the Pune region.

Academic Program

Bachelor of Physiotherapy (B.P.T.)

A premier 5-year undergraduate medical degree in physical medicine and rehabilitation

B.P.T. (Bachelor of Physiotherapy)

Affiliated to Maharashtra University of Health Sciences (MUHS), Nashik

4.5 Years + 6 Months Internship Undergraduate Medical Degree Code: 06413

Eligibility Criteria

12th Science (HSC) passed with Physics, Chemistry, Biology (PCB) and English with valid NEET-UG score as prescribed by State CET Cell Maharashtra.

Career Scope

Opportunities in Multi-speciality Hospitals, Sports Medicine & Fitness, Orthopedic & Neuro Rehab Clinics, Defense Forces, Research, and Private Practice.

Admission Quota

Admissions through State Common Entrance Test Cell (CET Cell) Maharashtra CAP Rounds, Institutional Quota & Stray Vacancy Rounds.

Core Departments & Training Wings

Musculoskeletal & Orthopedic

Specialized rehabilitation for fractures, joint replacements, spinal disorders, sports injuries, and arthritis.

Neuro-Physiotherapy

Restoring motor function in patients with Stroke (Paralysis), Parkinson's disease, Cerebral Palsy, and Spinal cord trauma.

Cardio-Respiratory Rehab

Critical ICU chest physiotherapy, post-cardiac surgery recovery, and pulmonary rehabilitation for asthma/COPD patients.

Kinesiotherapy & Biomechanics

Scientific motion study, therapeutic exercises, gait analysis, ergonomic corrections, and muscle re-education.

Campus Amenities

World-Class Healthcare & Learning Infrastructure

Providing an environment where students gain both theoretical mastery and clinical confidence

Electrotherapy OPD Unit

Equipped with advanced IFT, Ultrasound, TENS, Traction, Shortwave Diathermy (SWD), and Laser therapy modalities.

Exercise Therapy Gymnasium

Full-scale rehabilitation gym featuring parallel bars, quadriceps chairs, shoulder wheels, suspension therapy, and tilt tables.

Attached Multi-Speciality Hospital

Direct exposure to in-patient (IPD), out-patient (OPD), and Intensive Care Units (ICU) under specialist guidance.

Medical & Physical Therapy Library

Comprehensive repository of standard medical textbooks, international physiotherapy journals, and digital e-library access.
