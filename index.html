Bachelor of Physiotherapy (B.P.Th)

Intake: 60 Seats (Proposed) | MUHS Nashik & DMER Maharashtra Standards

The Bachelor of Physiotherapy (B.P.Th) is a premier 4.5-year health sciences degree program that trains students in diagnosing, treating, and preventing movement dysfunctions, musculoskeletal disorders, neurological impairments, and sports injuries. Students undergo 4 years of classroom and clinical instruction followed by 6 months of mandatory rotatory bedside hospital internship.

Eligibility Criteria (MUHS & DMER Norms):

• 10+2 / HSC Science Passed with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB).
• Minimum PCB aggregate: Open Category - 50%, Reserved Category - 40%.
• Candidate must have appeared in NEET-UG with a valid non-zero score.

Key Curriculum & Clinical Training Wings:
Human Anatomy & Physiology Kinesiology & Biomechanics Electrotherapy & Electrodiagnosis Musculoskeletal / Orthopedic Physiotherapy Neuro-Physiotherapy & Stroke Rehab Cardio-Pulmonary & ICU Physiotherapy Sports Physiotherapy & Injury Prevention Community Based Rehabilitation (CBR)
Career Opportunities: Multi-Specialty Hospital Physiotherapist, Consultant at Sports Rehab Centers & IPL/National Teams, Neuro-Rehab Specialist, Orthopedic Physiotherapy Clinics, Ergonomics Corporate Consultant, Private Physiotherapy Clinic Owner, Higher Studies (M.P.Th, Ph.D), International Licensing (USA/UK/Canada/Australia).

Clinical Bedside Instruction

Attached Multi-Specialty Hospital & Live OPD Postings

At Shiv Sangam Pune College of Physiotherapy, classroom learning is seamlessly integrated with daily bedside patient care. Under the direct guidance of experienced orthopedic surgeons, neurologists, and senior physiotherapists, our students gain invaluable hands-on clinical exposure.

Orthopedic & Joint Rehab

Post-fracture recovery, total knee/hip replacement rehab, cervical-lumbar spondylosis care.

Neurological Rehabilitation

Stroke / hemiplegia recovery, spinal cord injury rehab, Parkinson's & cerebral palsy management.

Sports Injury Clinic

Ligament tears (ACL/PCL), rotator cuff injuries, athlete biomechanics & fitness rehabilitation.

ICU & Cardiopulmonary Care

Chest physiotherapy, ventilator weaning assistance, post-bypass cardiac exercise programs.

Clinical Rotatory Training

6 Months Guaranteed Rotatory Internship

Every student rotates through In-Patient Departments (IPD), Out-Patient Departments (OPD), Intensive Care Units (ICU), and Community Rehabilitation camps to emerge as a fully confident clinician.
