Shiv Sangam Pune College of Physiotherapy

Vivekanand Yuvak Mandal (Latur / Pune - Registration No: MAHA/10600/Latur) is a premier educational and social development trust dedicated to public enlightenment, youth empowerment, and excellence in professional healthcare education. Guided by the nation-building philosophy of Swami Vivekananda, the trust established Shiv Sangam Pune College of Physiotherapy at Phursungi, Pune.

The institution features state-of-the-art electrotherapy suites, a full-scale kinesiotherapy gymnasium, dedicated orthopedic, neuro-rehab, pediatric and sports therapy wings, and attached multi-specialty hospital clinical postings providing students with extensive hands-on bedside patient care experience.

Eligibility Criteria

• 12th Science (HSC) passed with Physics, Chemistry, Biology (PCB) and English.
• Minimum PCB Aggregate: 50% for Open Category, 40% for Reserved Category.
• Valid non-zero score in NEET-UG entrance examination.

Career Scope

• Multi-Specialty Hospital Physiotherapist.
• Sports Rehabilitation Consultant (IPL, Sports Teams, Fitness Centers).
• Neuro-Rehab & Orthopedic Therapy Centers.
• Private Physical Therapy Clinic Consultant.
• Higher Education (M.P.Th, Ph.D) in India & Overseas.

Admission Process

• State CET Cell Maharashtra CAP Rounds.
• Institutional Level Quota & Stray Vacancy Counseling.
• All Government Scholarships (EBC, SC, ST, OBC, VJNT, SEBC, Minority) strictly applicable.

Musculoskeletal & Orthopedic

Rehabilitation for bone fractures, total joint replacements, spinal disorders, sports injuries, and arthritis.

Neuro-Physiotherapy

Motor recovery for Stroke (Paralysis), Parkinson's disease, Cerebral Palsy, and spinal cord injuries.

Cardio-Respiratory & ICU

Intensive care chest therapy, post-cardiac surgery mobilization, and pulmonary rehabilitation for COPD/Asthma.

Kinesiotherapy & Sports

Movement biomechanics, therapeutic exercise gymnasium, posture retraining, and athletic conditioning.
